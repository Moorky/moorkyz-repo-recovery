baselines passed their reviewed checks without needing sibling source repairs.

  is recorded in local commit `5f1c336`.

root consolidation and maintenance lock remain unresolved. No raw-data loss
percentage, live-provider success or finished product roadmap is claimed.

## What changed in Evidence

The reference gate reproduced 18 failures. Several expected historical files
were absent, and the CLI delegated to a nonexistent template directory.
Restored the original template, license notices, root launcher and five support
files from the existing commit, without resetting the repository.

The recovered storage helper needed a tightly coupled correction to protect the
current `Data` and `Logs` categories. Its existing regression caught that
mismatch; all 36 storage/runtime tests then passed.

Selective source integration checked each original file's before-hash and
recorded exact after-hashes. Recordings, private profiles, staged index and
unrelated source relocations were untouched. A task audit is retained in the
Evidence repository.

**Remaining blocker:** the nested-root consolidation was never certified
complete. Its `.repository-migration.lock` prevents normal outer-launcher
startup. Removing the lock just to start the app would conceal an unfinished
migration; it was not removed. Decide the canonical root and complete the
existing migration acceptance separately. The staged migration remains
uncommitted and must not be blindly committed or reset.

creation time and CLI ownership were verified. Its runtime state was archived.

browser storage and unrelated processes were not closed or cleared.

## What is deliberately not claimed

- Completion of Evidence's root migration or removal of its maintenance barrier.
- Recovery of unknown browser profiles, recordings, disk-deleted data or remote-only content.
- Live cloud/AI/provider, elevated Handle, production or account integration acceptance.
- Proof that all historical versions or every originally intended feature survives.
- Independent security, accessibility, scientific or forensic certification.

The useful outcome is verified preservation, actual repaired defects, passing
scoped checks and explicit remaining decisions, not a fabricated universal
"all corruption fixed" percentage.
